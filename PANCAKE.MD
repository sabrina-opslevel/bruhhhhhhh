# Pancake Recipe

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

## Instructions

1. Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together buttermilk, milk, egg, and melted butter.
3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined (lumps are fine).
4. Let batter rest for 5 minutes.
5. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease with butter or oil.
6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the skillet.
7. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and edges look set, about 2-3 minutes.
8. Flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.
9. Serve warm with maple syrup, butter, or your favorite toppings.

## Tips

- Do not overmix the batter; lumps are normal and lead to fluffier pancakes.
- Keep finished pancakes warm in a 200°F (93°C) oven while cooking the rest.
- For extra fluffy pancakes, separate the egg, beat the white to soft peaks, and fold into the batter last.
